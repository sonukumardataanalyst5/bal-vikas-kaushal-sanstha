Our Focus Areas

Four pillars, one promise.

From the first day of school to the first day at work — we stay with the child.

Child Education

School fees, books, tuition and mentoring so children stay in class and keep learning.

Skill Development

Kaushal Vikash — computers, tailoring and job skills that make young people self-reliant.

Health & Nutrition

Daily meals, health camps and hygiene kits — a healthy body carries a learning mind.

Child Safety & Protection

Child-rights awareness, counselling and safe spaces — every childhood deserves protection.
